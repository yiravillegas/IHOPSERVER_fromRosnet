# KPI methodology

| Metric | Source | Calculation |
|---|---|---|
| Beverage rate | Employee Contest, Eat In | Beverage sales / Eat In net sales |
| Discount rate | Employee Sales Statistics | Discount dollars / employee net sales |
| PPA | Employee Sales Statistics | Employee net sales / covers |
| Time | Server Table Turn Stats | Employee Avg Mins |
| Net sales | Employee Sales Statistics | Employee Net Sales; recognition only |

AM beverage target: 18.5%; PM: 17%. Beverage points: min(100, actual / target × 100).

Discount points: max(0, min(100, (7% − actual) / (7% − 3.5%) × 100)). Time points: min(100, 46 / Avg Mins × 100).

PPA target is the included team's total net sales divided by total covers, calculated separately each month. PPA points: min(100, individual PPA / team target × 100).

Weighted score: beverage × 0.40 + discount × 0.25 + time × 0.20 + PPA × 0.15. Do not round intermediate values.

Tier 1 requires score ≥95; Tier 2 ≥88; both require discount <3.5% and Avg Mins <46. Tier 3 applies to scores ≥78 that do not qualify for Tier 1/2. Tier 4 applies below 78 even when a threshold flag exists. This explicitly resolves an ambiguity in the original guide.

Only approved AM/PM servers are included. Online-order accounts and explicit exclusions do not affect team targets. Current configuration applies to both months when recalculating comparisons; archive monthly configuration if historical roster/targets must be preserved.

The time report is limited to its listed card payment categories and excludes accounts open longer than three hours. It does not directly establish seated-to-departure time. Discount totals do not identify causes. Sales volume is not adjusted for hours worked. No tip income or causal employee-performance claims are inferred.
