# AI-assisted restaurant reporting: portfolio case study

## Ownership and contribution

The project owner identified a recurring restaurant reporting problem, supplied the source reports, interpreted business definitions, specified shift targets and exclusions, and chose a weighted monthly PPA baseline. AI assisted data inspection, coding, workbook design and documentation. Calculations remain deterministic and auditable.

## Demonstrated skills

- Translating operational requirements into explicit calculation rules.
- Joining multiple reports while preserving their different scopes.
- Weighted aggregation, source reconciliation and exception handling.
- Iterative AI collaboration with corrections grounded in business knowledge.
- Excel reporting, readable operational summaries and reproducible code.
- Separating public demonstration assets from private employee data.

## Evidence and limitations

The original working project used six source files for a two-month comparison. Formula outputs were compared against independent calculations. The portable monthly calculator reproduced every prior score, tier and weighted PPA target. Five public unit tests use fictional employees. An app-managed monthly task is active in the private operational chat, with manual file attachments; its first future scheduled execution is not yet observed. Automatic Rosnet extraction and Telegram publishing are not implemented. No measured savings or revenue impact is claimed.

## Suggested résumé bullet

**English:** Developed an AI-assisted restaurant KPI reporting workflow that consolidated three monthly Rosnet exports, validated source totals, calculated shift-based performance scores and weighted PPA, and produced an auditable Excel workbook and Telegram-ready summaries.
